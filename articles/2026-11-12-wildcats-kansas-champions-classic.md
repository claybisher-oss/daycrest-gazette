Kentucky's first big test of the season didn't go its way. In the Champions Classic in Chicago on Tuesday night, No. 15 Kansas beat the 24th-ranked Wildcats 89-69.

## How it got away

Kentucky actually started well, jumping to a 10-5 lead behind early baskets from Zoom Diallo and Alex Wilkins. Kansas answered with a burst from freshman Tyran Stokes and led 46-36 at halftime. The game broke open early in the second half, when the Jayhawks scored **12 straight points** and never let Kentucky back in.

## The numbers that mattered

- **Tre White (Kansas):** a career-high 30 points.
- **Tyran Stokes (Kansas):** 23 points and 10 rebounds. He and White took more than half of Kansas's shots.
- **Zoom Diallo (Kentucky):** 24 points.
- **Malachi Moreno (Kentucky):** 19 points, 10 rebounds and 5 assists.

Beyond those two, Kentucky couldn't find steady scoring. Kansas has now won three straight in the series.

## Next up

Kentucky returns home to host **Northern Arizona on Friday, Nov. 13, at 7 p.m.** The Wildcats opened the season with an 85-57 win over Manhattan, in which Milan Momcilovic scored 24 in his debut.

## Why it matters

November losses to top teams don't decide a season, but they show where the work is. The question for this team is who becomes the reliable second and third scorer next to Diallo and Moreno before conference play arrives.
