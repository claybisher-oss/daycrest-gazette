A cold, gray Friday gives way to a bright, frosty weekend: good conditions for a few hours on the water or the trail.

## Fall trout

Kentucky Fish and Wildlife's November stockings finished on November 12 ([November schedule](https://fw.ky.gov/Fish/Pages/Monthly-Trout-Stocking.aspx); [fall lake schedule](https://fw.ky.gov/Fish/pages/planned-monthly-trout-stocking-schedule.aspx)). Close to Lexington this fall:

The agency describes its fall fish as roughly 10-inch, half-pound stockers. Small spinners, PowerBait or a nymph under an indicator all work in the park lakes.

## The Cumberland below Wolf Creek Dam

The Corps of Engineers' [six-day generation schedule](https://www.lrn-wc.usace.army.mil/rtos.shtml) shows steady daily totals this week (920 megawatt-hours a day, dropping to 665 on Saturday). KDFWR's [winter tailwater guide](https://fw.ky.gov/Fish/Pages/lake-cumberland-tailwater-offers-great-winter-trout-fishing.aspx) explains the code: "45" in the Wolf Creek column means one turbine, "90" means two, and more than two makes the upper river too high to fish. In low, clear water, go small: size 18–20 pheasant tails. Check the schedule the night before and again in the morning.

## Bass in cold water

- **Stream smallmouth:** once creek water drops below about 50°F, usually by mid-November, the bite slows sharply. Fish school up in pools, so one fish often means more in the same hole. Slow down, fish flat bedrock and make long, quiet casts in clear water ([KDFWR](https://fw.ky.gov/Fish/Pages/stream-smallmouth-bass-love-early-fall.aspx)).
- **Small-lake largemouth:** early November through early December can be surprisingly good. Bass follow baitfish into the backs of coves; small jigs near deep wood are a classic choice ([KDFWR](https://fw.ky.gov/Fish/Pages/largemouth-bass-active-on-smaller-lakes-across-Kentucky-.aspx)).

## License reminders

Kentucky's license year runs March 1 through February 28. A **trout permit** is required to keep or fish for trout and is included in the Sportsman's license ([license descriptions](https://fw.ky.gov/Licenses/Pages/License-and-Permit-Descriptions.aspx)). Catch-and-release trout streams are open October through March, artificial lures only.

## Weekend outdoors: Raven Run

Saturday (sunny, 46°) and Sunday (mostly sunny, 49°) are made for a walk. [Raven Run Nature Sanctuary](https://www.lexingtonky.gov/raven-run-nature-sanctuary) in southern Fayette County has about 10 miles of trail through meadows and woods down toward the Kentucky River palisades. Winter hours are 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. daily, and the gate closes temporarily when parking fills, so go early. Expect mud after Friday's showers, and remember it's the opening weekend of gun deer season: wear something bright anywhere near private land.

## Why it matters

Late fall rewards anglers who fish slow and small, and the weekend weather is the best of the week. A morning at a stocked park lake and an afternoon on the palisades trails is about as good as mid-November gets.
