Kentucky's busiest two weeks in the woods start this weekend. Kentucky Department of Fish and Wildlife Resources officers used WKYT to remind hunters of the basics before modern gun deer season opens.

## The essentials

- **Dates:** Saturday, November 14, through Sunday, November 29.
- **Paperwork:** a valid hunting license, plus a statewide deer permit (or youth deer permit). Together those allow up to four deer.
- **Hours:** legal shooting time runs from 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset. With sunrise around 7:16 and sunset around 5:28 in Lexington this weekend, that's roughly 6:45 a.m. to 6 p.m.
- **Hunter orange:** hunters, and anyone going along with them, must wear solid, unbroken hunter orange on the head, back and chest, visible from all sides.

## For everyone else

Even if you don't hunt, these two weeks change the countryside:

- Expect more trucks parked along back roads at dawn and dusk.
- Deer move more when pressured, so drive carefully at those hours; November is already peak season for deer–vehicle collisions.
- If you walk fields, farm lanes or wooded edges, wear something bright orange too. Many public trails are fine, but private land and wildlife-management areas will be busy.

## Why it matters

Deer season is a big part of rural Kentucky life and of how the state manages a large deer herd. A few simple habits (orange clothing, slow driving at dusk, knowing the dates) keep everyone safe.
