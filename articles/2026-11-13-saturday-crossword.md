## How to play

- **Tap a square** to start; tap the same square again to switch between Across and Down.
- **Type** to fill letters (on a phone, the keyboard opens when you tap). Arrow keys move around on a computer; Backspace erases.
- **Check** marks wrong letters in red. **Reveal** fills in the whole grid (it asks first).

## About the puzzle

Every entry is a common English word, every square is part of both an Across and a Down answer, and the black squares are symmetric when the grid is turned upside down, the classic American crossword rules. This first one leans easy, with short words throughout; future puzzles will stretch a little. Answers run in Monday's issue.

## Why it matters

A crossword is a small, screen-light way to end the week: ten or fifteen minutes of thinking about words instead of scrolling. It works fine on paper too; print the page or copy the grid onto a notepad.
