Both of Kentucky's marquee teams come home this weekend looking to bounce back.

## Basketball: Northern Arizona, tonight

- **When:** Friday, Nov. 13, 7 p.m. ET, Rupp Arena
- **TV:** SEC Network+ (streaming)

Kentucky (2–1) lost 89–69 to No. 15 Kansas on Tuesday at the Champions Classic in Chicago. Kansas led by 10 at the half and never let Kentucky get closer than that after the break. The telling number: Kansas scored **22 points off turnovers; Kentucky scored none**. Zoom Diallo led UK with 24 points, and Malachi Moreno posted a career-high 19 points with 10 rebounds and 5 assists. Kansas's Tre White scored a career-best 30, 23 of them after halftime. UK made only 4 of 21 three-pointers, according to KU Sports.

**Coming up:** Grambling State, Mon., Nov. 16, 7 p.m. (SECN+, Rupp); Indiana in Indianapolis, Fri., Nov. 20, 8 p.m. (CBS). ([schedule](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/kentucky-basketball-schedule-2026-27-190058712.html))

## Football: Florida, Saturday night

- **When:** Saturday, Nov. 14, 6:15 p.m. ET, Kroger Field ([kickoff announcement](https://floridagators.com/news/2026/11/8/football-florida-kentucky-game-set-for-6-15-kick-on-espn))
- **TV:** ESPN

Kentucky's six-game winning streak ended last Saturday with a 34–6 loss at Tennessee ([recap](https://local12.com/sports/uk-wildcats/no-8-kentuckys-6-game-win-steak-snapped-in-34-6-loss-at-no-16-tennessee)). The Vols' defense piled up eight tackles for loss and forced three turnovers. Quarterback Kenny Minchey threw for 224 yards and DeSean Bishop ran for 109. Kentucky is 7–2 (5–2 SEC), with three regular-season games left: Florida, at Missouri (Nov. 21) and Louisville at home (Nov. 28). Rankings from before the Tennessee game will likely shift this week.

## Why it matters

Home games after a loss tell you a lot. For basketball, watch whether ball security improves. For football, a win over Florida keeps a strong season on track heading into the Louisville finale.
