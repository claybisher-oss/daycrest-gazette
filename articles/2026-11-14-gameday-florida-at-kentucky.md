Wins over FAU, Campbell, at Auburn (44–39), Ole Miss (52–28) and at Texas (27–24); losses at Missouri, to South Carolina, Georgia and Oklahoma (23–7 last week, when Jones went 10 of 22 with two interceptions) ([cfbstats](https://cfbstats.com/2026/team/235/index.html)). ## History

Florida leads the series 54–21, but Kentucky has won three of the last four: 26–16 (2022), 33–14 (2023) and 38–7 last November in Lexington; Florida won 48–20 in 2024 ([Winsipedia](https://www.winsipedia.com/games/florida/vs/kentucky/2026/127694)). For decades this was a lopsided rivalry — Florida's long winning streak in the series ended in 2018 — so recent Kentucky wins are a real shift.

## Learn one thing: why third down decides games

![Yard lines every five yards: a first down means covering two of these stripes](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1575317988650-5faf389cabef?ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&fm=jpg&crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&w=1000)
*Photo by [Joshua Hanson](https://unsplash.com/@joshuahanson43?utm_source=daycrest_gazette&utm_medium=referral) on [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/?utm_source=daycrest_gazette&utm_medium=referral)*

![Watch a drive: the solid line is the ball, the dashed line is the line to gain; third down decides whether the drive lives](img/2026-11-14-third-down.gif)

A team gets four tries ("downs") to gain 10 yards. Most teams use the first three to move the ball and punt on fourth. So **third down** is the money down: convert it and the drive lives; fail and you usually give the ball away.

Kentucky's offense converts only about 35% of its third downs, while its defense allows opponents to convert nearly 48% — the wrong side of both numbers. Tonight, watch **"third and short" (1–3 yards)** vs. **"third and long" (7+)**. Good offenses stay out of third-and-long by gaining 4+ yards on first down. If Kentucky's defense keeps Florida in third-and-long and its offense keeps third downs short, Kentucky is probably winning.

**Try it tonight:** on each Kentucky third down, note the distance and whether they made it. By halftime you'll know who's controlling the game.

## Why it matters

A home win keeps Kentucky's best season in years — and its first real playoff hope — alive heading into two November rivalry games. A loss after Tennessee would turn a breakout year into a slide. Florida, playing for bowl eligibility under a coach who knows Lexington well, won't make it easy.
