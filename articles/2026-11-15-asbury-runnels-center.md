Asbury Theological Seminary in Wilmore announced on Nov. 10 a new Runnels Family Center for Political and Public Theology.

## What it is
The seminary describes the center as non-partisan. Its aim is to bring a Wesleyan theological voice to public questions such as human dignity, immigration, the sanctity of life and religious liberty.

## Who is behind it
A legacy gift from the Runnels family funds the center, and Dr. Asa James Swan will lead it. The seminary plans a full launch in 2027.

## Why it matters
Wesleyan theology has long joined personal holiness with social holiness. A center at one of the movement's largest seminaries could give churches in Kentucky and beyond a steadier, less partisan way to think through public life.
