## Holiday markets
WEKU [rounded up](https://www.weku.org/lexington-richmond/2026-11-13/want-to-shop-local-this-year-here-are-some-holiday-markets-in-central-kentucky-to-check-out) dozens of holiday markets running from mid-November through December, from church markets in Nicholasville to events in Lexington, Midway, Versailles, Berea and Frankfort. Coming up this week: the Midway European Christmas Market (Fri., 6-9 PM), the MixMas market at Manchester Music Hall in Lexington (Fri.-Sat.), the Kentucky Castle market in Versailles (Sat.-Sun.) and a holiday market at Fritz Farm (Sun., 1-5 PM).

## America 250 in Jessamine County
Historian and author Kent Masterson Brown speaks at the county's [America 250 event](https://jessamineky.gov/event/celebrate-america-250-with-kent-masterson-brown/) this afternoon, 2-4 PM, at East Jessamine High School.

## Why it matters
Shopping local keeps holiday dollars with Central Kentucky makers, and the America 250 series is a free way to learn local history ahead of the nation's 250th birthday in 2026.
