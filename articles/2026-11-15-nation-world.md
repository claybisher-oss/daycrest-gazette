## What caused the ground stop
NPR [reports](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/14/nx-s1-6040952/faa-ground-stop-airplane-travel) that Friday's four-hour stop on U.S. departures came from an equipment error while a contractor replaced old copper lines with fiber at a remote FAA site. Officials said they saw no sign of anything malicious. More than 9,000 flights were canceled Friday, and airlines expect schedules to be back to normal by the end of today.

## Starship comes home
SpaceX for the first time [caught](https://www.weku.org/npr-news/2026-11-14/spacex-successfully-catches-starship-and-makes-history) a Starship upper stage in the arms of its Texas launch tower, about 22 hours after launch. The ship reached orbit and released 26 satellites. The first-stage booster exploded shortly after liftoff, so the planned catch of both stages fell short.

## Why it matters
If you fly this week, check your flight's status before heading to the airport. Reusable rockets that land back at the pad could sharply cut the cost of reaching orbit.
