## Today's readings
Revised Common Lectionary, Proper 28, Year A (Track 2; Track 1 reads Judges 4:1-7 and Psalm 123): Zephaniah 1:7, 12-18 · Psalm 90:1-8, 12 · 1 Thessalonians 5:1-11 · Matthew 25:14-30.

## The gospel in brief
Before a long journey, a master entrusts his property to three servants, "each according to his ability." Two put the money to work and double it; both hear the same welcome into their master's joy. The third, afraid of his master, buries his share and returns exactly what he was given. A "talent" was an enormous sum, many years of a laborer's wages.

## What to notice
- The two faithful servants receive different amounts but the *same* praise.
- The third servant's problem is fear and a false picture of the master, not a small gift.
- Paul's reading this week (1 Thessalonians 5) sets the scene: the day of the Lord comes unexpectedly, so live awake and sober, encouraging one another.

## The collect
The Book of Common Prayer (1979) prays this week that we may "read, mark, learn, and inwardly digest" the Scriptures and hold fast the hope of everlasting life.

## Why it matters
Near the end of the church year, the readings turn to endings and accountability. The parable answers with trust: what we have is entrusted, and faithfulness, not anxiety, is the response.
