On Nov. 15, 1917, Oswald Chambers died in Egypt, where he had been serving as a YMCA chaplain to British and Commonwealth troops during World War I.

## The man
A Scottish Bible teacher, Chambers had led a small Bible training college in London before going to Egypt in 1915. He taught soldiers in a desert camp near Cairo.

## The book he never wrote
His wife, Gertrude "Biddy" Chambers, had taken down his talks in shorthand. After his death she spent years editing them into books, most famously *My Utmost for His Highest* (1927), a daily devotional still widely read.

## Why it matters
Most of Chambers's influence came after his death, through faithful work by someone else: a fitting story for a Sunday when the gospel is about stewarding what is entrusted.
