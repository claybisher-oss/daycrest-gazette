## Football: Florida 21, Kentucky 14
Kentucky led 7-0 on a short touchdown pass, but a holding penalty in its own end zone gave Florida a safety. Florida's touchdown run and two-point try with 9:08 left won it, [WKYT reports](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/15/florida-beats-no-13-kentucky-21-14-sumralls-return-lexington/). Florida outgained UK 330-242, Kentucky ran for only 61 yards, and the Cats turned it over on downs at the Florida 25 with 5:29 left. Kentucky falls to 7-3 (5-3 SEC) with a second straight loss.

A Sea of Blue's [three takeaways](https://www.aseaofblue.com/kentucky-wildcats-football/188021/florida-gators-final-score-3-things-to-know-takeaways-uk): the offense stalled, the tight end matters more to this attack than it looks, and playoff hopes are likely gone.

**Next:** at Missouri, Sat., Nov. 21, 7:30 PM ET, SEC Network ([schedule](https://fbschedules.com/college-football-schedule-tv-selections-for-november-21-2026/)); the regular season ends at home against Louisville.

## Basketball: a last-second win
Alex Wilkins hit a three with 2.2 seconds left to beat Northern Arizona 78-77 on Friday at Rupp; three Cats scored 14 each ([UK Athletics](https://ukathletics.com/news/2026/11/13/wilkins-clutch-shot-lifts-kentucky-past-northern-arizona/)). UK is 3-1.

**Next:** Grambling State, Mon., Nov. 16, 7 PM ET, SEC Network+; then Indiana in Indianapolis, Fri., Nov. 20, 8 PM.

## Why it matters
Two losses in a row cool football's postseason picture; the last two games decide bowl positioning. Basketball has a softer Monday before a big neutral-site test with Indiana.
