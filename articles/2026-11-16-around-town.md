- **Holiday Greenery Helpers** — Shaker Village of Pleasant Hill, Nov 20 (3 p.m.) and Nov 21 (2 and 3 p.m.). Make a fresh-greenery swag; ages 8+, $10, tickets required. [Details](https://shakervillageky.org/events/holiday-greenery-helpers-2026/)
- **Library storytimes** — Jessamine County Public Library, Nicholasville: Mother Goose (Mon), Family (Tue), Bilingual Preschool (Wed), 10:30 a.m.; Pokémon Club Sat 10:30 a.m. [Details](https://jessaminejournal.com/2026/11/11/upcoming-events-at-the-jessamine-county-public-library-24/)
- **Irving Berlin's White Christmas** — Lexington Theatre Company, Lexington Opera House, Nov 19-22. [Guide](https://smileypete.com/community/2026-holiday-events/)
- **MixMas Market** — Manchester Music Hall, Fri Nov 20 5-9 p.m., Sat Nov 21 9 a.m.-3 p.m. [Guide](https://smileypete.com/community/2026-holiday-events/)
- **Southern Lights** — Kentucky Horse Park, opens Nov 27, nightly 5:30-10 p.m., $35 per carload. [Details](https://www.khpfoundation.org/southern-lights/)
