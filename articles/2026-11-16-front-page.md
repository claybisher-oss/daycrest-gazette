## A pastor's long trip home
A Pikeville pastor and hospital chaplain was caught at Chicago's O'Hare when Friday's nationwide flight halt began. He eventually flew into Blue Grass Airport and drove roughly two and a half hours home, arriving in time to preach Sunday. ([WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/15/kentucky-pastor-describes-stressful-trip-home-after-nationwide-flight-grounding/))

## Fatal shooting on Beaver Place
Lexington police responded to the 3500 block of Beaver Place around 8:30 p.m. Saturday and found an adult with a gunshot wound who died at the scene. No suspect information has been released. ([WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/16/coroner-scene-lexington-apartment-complex/))

## Duplex fire on Carlisle Avenue
A Friday fire at a duplex in the 200 block of Carlisle Avenue was put out quickly. One person was hospitalized with non-life-threatening injuries and four residents were displaced; the cause is under investigation. ([LEX 18](https://www.lex18.com/news/covering-kentucky/4-people-displaced-1-injured-after-fire-at-lexington-duplex))

## Why it matters
Air travel is still recovering this week — anyone flying out of LEX should check their flight before leaving home.
