## Meet Grambling State
- **Program:** the Tigers play in the SWAC under second-year coach **Patrick Crarey II**. They went 14-19 last season.
- **Season so far:**
  - Opened by beating Northern Illinois 89-87 in a game that tipped at **3 a.m.**, part of college basketball's opening-day marathon.
  - Lost 93-62 at Texas on Nov 9.
- **Players to know:**
  - **Isaiah Miranda** is a 7-footer and LIU transfer averaging 18.5 points, 6 rebounds and 2 blocks. He had 29 points against NIU but went 0 of 6 from three at Texas.
  - **Dwayne Pierce** is a guard averaging 13 points and 4.5 assists.
  - **Mekhi Fitts** averages 12.5 points.
- **Style:** a guard-driven offense. The defense gives up about 90 points a game and opponents hit 42.6% of their threes (349th nationally). Grambling's rebounding ranks around 301st.
- **History:** Grambling made its **first NCAA Tournament in 2024**. It won a First Four game against Montana State in overtime, then lost to Purdue.

## Learn one thing: the five positions
Basketball positions are numbered 1 through 5, and broadcasters use the numbers constantly ("he can play the 4").
- **1, point guard:** brings the ball up and calls the plays.
- **2, shooting guard:** a scorer on the wing.
- **3, small forward:** a wing who can shoot and drive.
- **4, power forward:** a bigger player who rebounds and, in modern offenses, often shoots from outside.
- **5, center:** guards the rim, sets screens and rebounds.

Kentucky blurs these lines. Moreno is a 7-foot 5 who averages almost 4 assists, so the offense often runs through him at the top of the key. Momcilovic is a 6-9 4 who lives behind the three-point line.

**Try it tonight:** on three possessions, find who brings the ball up (the 1) and where Moreno stands when the play starts. Then count Kentucky's offensive rebounds by halftime; after the NAU game, anything over five is progress.

## Trivia to bring to Rupp
- Kentucky has the most wins of any program in college basketball history. It also has 8 national titles (1948, 1949, 1951, 1958, 1978, 1996, 1998, 2012) and 17 Final Fours. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky_basketball))
- Rupp opened in 1976 and has seated 20,545 since its 2019 renovation. UK is 529-64 there all-time and has won a record 25 national attendance titles.
- Moreno is the first Wildcat with at least 14 points, 6 rebounds and 5 assists in back-to-back games since Shai Gilgeous-Alexander in March 2018.
- The NAU game was UK's first one-point win at Rupp since beating Texas A&M 74-73 in January 2018.
- Pope's teams are 30-1 when they shoot 50% or better from the field. UK has done it in 3 of 4 games this season. ([UK notes](https://ukathletics.com/news/2026/11/13/kentucky-northern-arizona-postgame-notes/))
