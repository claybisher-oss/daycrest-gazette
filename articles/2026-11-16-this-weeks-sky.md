- **Tue Nov 17:** first quarter moon (6:48 a.m. EST).
- **Night of Nov 17-18:** Leonid meteors peak. Expect 10-15 an hour from a dark spot after midnight; the moon sets around midnight, so the sky stays dark.
- **Before dawn:** Jupiter and Mars sit close together near Regulus; Venus is low in the east-southeast near Spica; Mercury is low in the east, best around Nov 20.
- **Evenings:** Saturn in the south; the moon passes it Nov 19-20. Full Beaver Moon Nov 24.

## Why it matters
Clear, cold nights this week make for good viewing — step outside after midnight Tuesday and give your eyes 20 minutes.
