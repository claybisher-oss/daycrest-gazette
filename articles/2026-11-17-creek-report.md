## Where the trout are going
Kentucky Fish & Wildlife's fall program stocks in October and November, then again in February and March. November waters near the Bluegrass include Rolex Lake at the Kentucky Horse Park (1,500 trout), Millennium Park Pond in Danville (500), Anderson County Community Park Lake (500) and Logan Hubble Park Lake in Lincoln County (1,500). Jessamine County's lakes were stocked in October and are next on the list in February. [KDFWR schedule](https://fw.ky.gov/Fish/pages/planned-monthly-trout-stocking-schedule.aspx)

## Regulation changes
Changes effective Nov. 5 left trout rules alone. On the fishing side, landowners may move fish from their own private ponds and skip limits for nontraditional methods there, and can extend that in writing to licensed anglers. Deer, furbearer and waterfowl-area rules also changed. [KDFWR](https://fw.ky.gov/News/Pages/Kentucky-Fish-and-Wildlife-provides-recap-of-recently-amended-regulations-nov-2026.aspx)

## Tailwater tip
On the Cumberland below Wolf Creek Dam, the river rises fast when the dam generates. Call the schedule line, (606) 678-8697, before wading. [KDFWR](https://fw.ky.gov/Education/Pages/Cumberland-River-Tailwater.aspx)

## Why it matters
Freshly stocked trout bite best in the first days and weeks after release — a short, cold-weather trip close to home is the easiest fishing of the season.
