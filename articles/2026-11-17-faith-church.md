## Jordan's Christians grieve
Christianity Today reports on how Jordanian Christians are responding to the October 23 gun attack on St. George's Greek Orthodox Church in Karak, which killed nine people. On Nov. 15 a Jordanian court sentenced three men to death for the attack, according to [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/15/jordan-sentences-three-men-to-death-by-hanging-after-deadly-church-attack). [Christianity Today](https://www.christianitytoday.com/2026/11/jordanian-christians-grieve-terror-attack-on-karak-church/)

## AI and the Bible
YouVersion's CEO says even leading AI models misquote Scripture at least 15 percent of the time, and some far more often — a real problem for a Bible app. The practical lesson for any reader: check a verse against an actual Bible before you rely on it. [Christianity Today](https://www.christianitytoday.com/2026/11/youversion-bible-app-ai-claude-chatgpt-bobby-gruenewald/)

## Why it matters
One story asks us to pray for a suffering church; the other is a reminder that the text itself deserves careful handling.
