## Routine post-election reviews in 12 counties
State law requires the Attorney General's office to review election proceedings in randomly selected counties after every election. Investigators will interview officials and voters and examine records, then report to a grand jury in each county. Jessamine and Fayette were not drawn. The office says selection implies no wrongdoing. [WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/16/kentucky-attorney-generals-office-review-election-proceedings-12-randomly-selected-counties/)

## Comer announces run for governor
Republican U.S. Rep. James Comer announced Monday night, during a tele-town hall, that he is running for governor of Kentucky. The state's next governor's race is in 2027. [WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/video/2026/11/16/rep-james-comer-announces-run-kentucky-governor/)

## Kentucky across the Grammy ballot
Lexington-born Chris Stapleton, Oldham County metal band Knocked Loose and Billy Strings, who spent part of his childhood in Morehead, all earned nominations. The ceremony is Feb. 7, 2027. [WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/16/kentucky-shows-up-across-genres-this-years-grammy-nominations/)
