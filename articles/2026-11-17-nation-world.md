## Trump heads to Asia
President Trump left for the ASEAN summit in Manila, where he is to meet Quad leaders and sign a trade deal with Thailand, before traveling to China for a three-way meeting with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin. The trip comes as officials field questions following the resignation of Nvidia's CEO; NPR notes it has not independently confirmed intelligence claims reported around that story. [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/16/nx-s1-6041738/trump-xi-and-putin-china-nvidia)

## Rules for "killer robots"?
Countries meeting in Geneva are debating limits on weapons that can select and strike targets without a human decision. The U.N., the Red Cross and the Pope back binding rules. The U.S., Russia and Israel oppose them; Washington has proposed five years of voluntary guidelines instead. [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/16/nx-s1-6042548/un-killer-robots-ai-weapons-drones)

## Why it matters
Both stories turn on who sets the rules — for trade among great powers, and for machines making life-and-death choices.
