## Basketball: Kentucky 88, Grambling State 59
UK took over after halftime at Rupp Arena. Malachi Moreno had 22 points, 12 rebounds, 4 steals and 3 blocks; Zoom Diallo, Milan Momcilovic and Mason Williams scored 12 each, and Momcilovic nearly had a triple-double with 9 rebounds and 9 assists. [Fox Sports / AP](https://www.foxsports.com/articles/cbk/malachi-morenos-22-points-lead-kentuckys-drubbing-of-grambling)

**Next:** Indiana, Friday, Nov. 20, 8 p.m. ET on CBS at Lucas Oil Stadium in Indianapolis. [Schedule](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/kentucky-basketball-schedule-2026-27-190058712.html)

## Football: Florida 21, Kentucky 14
UK fell to 7-3 (5-3 SEC), its second straight loss, and dropped from No. 13 to No. 17 in the AP poll. Kenny Minchey threw for 181 yards and a touchdown and ran for another. **Next:** at Missouri, Saturday, Nov. 21, 7:30 p.m. ET, SEC Network.
