## Hilda of Whitby
Hilda founded and led the abbey at Whitby on the Yorkshire coast. Bede, writing about fifty years later, says people from ordinary folk to kings came to her for counsel, and that she taught those in her care to keep peace and charity above all. Her abbey hosted the Synod of Whitby in 664. It was also where a shy cowherd named Cædmon began to sing praise to God — the first English hymn writer we know by name, and Hilda welcomed him into the community.
