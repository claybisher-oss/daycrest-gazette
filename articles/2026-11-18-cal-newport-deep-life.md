Cal Newport announced (Nov 16) that his next book, *The Deep Life: A Structured Approach*, is available to pre-order ahead of a March release.

## Where it comes from
The idea traces to his March 2020 essay "The Deep Life: Some Notes," which described focusing with intention on what matters most — in work, at home and in the soul.

## What the book does
- Helps you **identify what resonates** for you.
- **Refines it into a concrete vision.**
- **Moves you toward it** step by step.

Newport calls this the foundation underneath his productivity and technology writing. Oliver Burkeman, who read an early copy, praised it as practical.

## Why it matters
The post itself is an announcement, not an argument. But the theme — a few deep things done well over many shallow ones — is close to the aim of this paper's own morning pages.
