## Why the East objected
Two reasons: the West changed a creed all the churches shared without an ecumenical council, and the phrase seemed to blur the Father as the one source within the Trinity. Western theologians answered that the addition guarded the Son's full equality with the Father. Many Protestant churches inherited the Western wording.
