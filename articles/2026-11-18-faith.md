## In brief
- The U.S. Conference of Catholic Bishops meets in Baltimore through Thursday.
- Its president, Archbishop Paul Coakley, said the Church should be understood by its mission, not in political terms.
- The papal nuncio encouraged the bishops to reach out to people who have drifted from the Church.

## What was said
Coakley named family life, worship, listening across the Church and faithful civic life as priorities for the coming year. Archbishop Caccia, the Vatican's ambassador, urged the bishops toward those who have stepped away.
