## A choir's song comes full circle
Nashville singer-songwriter Anna Vaus heard Frederick Douglass High School's choir sing her song "There Is Time" at its fall concert and asked to record it with them. A two-hour rehearsal and recording session was set for Wednesday in the school auditorium. [WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/17/lexington-high-school-choir-record-song-with-singer-songwriter-anna-vaus/)

## Fayette schools finish family listening sessions
Fayette County Public Schools held the last of its "Family Conversation" sessions at Tates Creek High School. Parents raised class sizes, specialized programs and how the district communicates with families; the acting superintendent said the feedback will help plan future school years. [WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/18/fcps-hold-final-family-conversation-event/)

## Brief: arson charge after duplex fire
Lexington police charged a 69-year-old man with first-degree arson and wanton endangerment after a Nov. 13 fire at a Carlisle Avenue duplex. A bystander helped a woman get out; one person was treated for injuries that were not life-threatening.
