## Why read it
*Jayber Crow* is narrated by the barber of Port William, Wendell Berry's fictional Kentucky river town. Looking back across a long life, he tells of a failed start at seminary, a lifetime of quiet service, and a faithful, unspoken love. It is slow in the best way, often funny, and asks what it means to belong to a place and its people. A good book for winter evenings.
