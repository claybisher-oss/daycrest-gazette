J.D. Walt's daily devotional on Romans 14 (Nov 17) makes a simple claim: Christian relationships are not a side issue to the mission — they *are* the mission.

## The argument
He starts with Jesus' prayer in John 17:20–21, that his followers would be one "so that the world may believe," and with 1 John 4:16, God is love. If the world's faith depends partly on how believers treat each other, a church whose relationships contradict the Bible gives people a reason not to believe.

## Three points
- **The bond of peace (Eph 4:1–3)** is strong because it rests on God's love, and fragile because we are broken.
- **Conflict will come.** What matters is how we engage it. Peace is always available, even when full reconciliation isn't.
- **God plays the long game.** His truth wins even when churches fracture. Walt's line: "Depart throwing roses, never rocks."

## Why it matters
A practical word for any strained relationship at home, church or work. The devotional ends with a prayer and journal questions — good material for an examen.
