J.D. Walt's devotional for Nov 16 takes up an old Christian saying: "In essentials, unity; in nonessentials, liberty; in all things, charity." The hard part, he says, is telling which is which.

## Paul's test
In Romans 14:13–18 Paul treats food and drink as nonessentials. But the conversation doesn't stop at "you're free." The real test is whether using your freedom harms someone else.

## Three points
- **A freedom can turn into sin** when it makes another believer stumble. Giving up a right for someone else's sake can be the right thing.
- **Stop judging** one another, and resolve to put no stumbling block in anyone's way.
- **Alcohol** is Walt's live example, given how common addiction is inside and outside the church. His summary: "The question is not whether it is legal but whether it is loving."

## Why it matters
A useful lens for any area of liberty — what you drink, watch or post. It ends with a prayer against judgmental rigidity and journal questions.
