## How to know it
Soft gray above, pale below, peach-orange flanks, and a little gray crest it can raise or flatten. It often travels with chickadees in mixed winter flocks. Hear it at [All About Birds](https://www.allaboutbirds.org/guide/Tufted_Titmouse/sounds).

## Where to find it
Any wooded yard or park with oaks. Watch a sunflower feeder: it grabs one seed, flies off, and hammers it open against a branch.

## Fun fact
Titmice stash seeds in bark crevices to eat later, and in spring they will pluck hair from a sleeping dog to line their nests.

## Why it matters
An easy first bird to learn by sight and sound — proof that a feeder and a few trees bring wild things right to the window.
