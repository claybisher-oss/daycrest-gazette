## Elizabeth of Hungary
A king's daughter, married young into the ruling family of Thuringia, Elizabeth gave away grain from the family stores during a famine and, after her husband's death, built a hospital at Marburg where she cared for the sick herself. She died in 1231 at twenty-four and was canonized only four years later. Her life is a picture of mercy given up close rather than from a distance.
