## The idea
Walter Brueggemann's *Sabbath as Resistance* (2014) argues that a weekly rest pushes back against a culture that measures people by output. Abraham Heschel called the Sabbath "a palace in time" (*The Sabbath*, 1951).

## Roots
Exodus 20:8–11 grounds the Sabbath in creation: God rested. Deuteronomy 5:12–15 grounds it in freedom: slaves in Egypt never got a day off. Christians moved the weekly rest to the Lord's Day, the day of the resurrection, keeping the gift of rest itself.

## Try it
One 24-hour stretch this week with no work email, no shopping, and one thing that is pure delight with the people you love.

## A question
What would you have to stop doing for one day to show you trust God with the other six?
