## Measles: U.S. loses elimination status
International health officials say the United States has lost measles-elimination status, with the virus at its highest rate in more than 30 years. Kentucky has 216 cases this year and 17 hospitalizations, concentrated in Christian, Laurel and Trigg counties. A good week to check your family's vaccine records with your doctor. [LEX 18](https://www.lex18.com/news/covering-kentucky/measles-status-kentucky-update-nov-17-2026)

## Ukraine: Kerch Bridge damaged; talks in Berlin
A section of the bridge linking Russia to Crimea collapsed after a Ukrainian operation; the Kremlin says it will rebuild. U.S. envoys are to meet Ukrainian and European officials in Berlin on Thursday, while a Kremlin aide says Russia has not agreed to European participation. [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw772jk4rvmxo) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/17/witkoff-kushner-ukraine-europe-russia-berlin-meeting)

## Gulf: drones at a U.S. radar in Bahrain
Iran's Revolutionary Guard said it targeted a radar at Bahrain's Sheikh Isa air base; Bahrain said it intercepted the attack, with no casualties or damage reported. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/oman-iran-foreign-ministers-discuss-safeguarding-maritime-corridors-2026-11-17/)
