## Asbury University's equine program gets a classroom hub
Asbury University in Wilmore is expanding its equine facilities with a new building called the Mama Jim Ranch House, named for Mary Jim Fuller Luce, a longtime supporter of the program. It will hold two classrooms, an apartment and office space. The program already cares for more than 100 horses across four main barns and enrolls more than 100 equine students; department chair Melissa Altman told LEX 18 the building will add indoor teaching room, especially in the colder months. Donor gifts paid for it — the university calls the project fully funded — with groundbreaking expected in spring 2027 and the building in full use by spring 2028. [LEX 18, Nov. 18](https://www.lex18.com/news/covering-kentucky/asbury-university-receives-funding-for-new-equine-facility-elevating-students-education)

## DV8 Kitchen asks for holiday gift cards
DV8 Kitchen, the Lexington restaurant that employs people in recovery from addiction, is asking the community to donate $25 gift cards — gas, department store or restaurant — for its employees this holiday season. Cards can be dropped off at the East End or South Broadway locations. Many of the employees are single parents, and the restaurant says the goal is to ease holiday money pressure. [LEX 18, Nov. 19](https://www.lex18.com/news/covering-kentucky/dv8-kitchen-asking-community-to-donate-gift-cards-to-support-employees-this-holiday-season)

## Why it matters
Both are small, concrete pieces of the region's life: a Jessamine County school investing in one of its signature programs, and a Lexington business built around second chances asking neighbors for a simple, practical kind of help.
