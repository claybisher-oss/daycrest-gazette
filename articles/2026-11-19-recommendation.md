## Why listen
Written after the death of Stevens's mother, *Carrie & Lowell* is spare — mostly voice, acoustic guitar and banjo — and asks hard questions about loss, memory and faith without easy answers. Scripture and church imagery run all through it. Put it on for an evening walk or the drive home, start to finish. (One song has a mild profanity.)
