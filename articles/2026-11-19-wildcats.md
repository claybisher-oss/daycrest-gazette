## Basketball: Kentucky vs. Indiana
The old rivals meet Friday night in Indianapolis (CBS). Indiana, in its second season under Darian DeVries, is 5–0 after an 81–76 win over No. 20 Arkansas at Madison Square Garden. Watch the glass: the Hoosiers have grabbed about 42% of their own misses. Kentucky won last season's meeting 72–60 in Rupp and leads the all-time series 33–25. Can Kentucky keep Indiana off the offensive boards? Second chances are where the Hoosiers have won their games.
