## Edmund of East Anglia
Edmund was a young Christian king in eastern England when a great Danish army overran his kingdom in 869–870. Early accounts say that, defeated and captured, he refused to rule as a puppet under a pagan overlord or to give up his faith, and he was killed. Within a generation his people were honoring him as a martyr, and the town of Bury St Edmunds grew up around his shrine.
