## Winter trout, all released
Each fall Kentucky stocks a set of small streams that run as catch-and-release fisheries from Oct. 1 through March 31 (Swift Camp Creek in Wolfe County stays catch-and-release until May 31). During the season you can't keep trout, and only artificial lures are allowed — no paste or dough baits. Closest to Central Kentucky: Swift Camp Creek in the Red River Gorge and the East Fork of Indian Creek in Menifee County. Fish-friendly habits help these fish last the winter: barbless hooks, wet hands, quick release. Check the [current trout stocking schedule](https://fw.ky.gov/Fish/Pages/Monthly-Trout-Stocking.aspx) and this year's fishing guide before you go, since the list can change.
