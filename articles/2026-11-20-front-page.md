## Lexington names a new fire chief
Mayor Linda Gorton named assistant chief Joey Harris, a Lexington native with 25 years in the department, as the city's next fire chief. He follows Jason Wells, who announced his retirement in September after three decades; Wells's retirement takes effect Jan. 10, 2027. [WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/19/lexington-mayor-announces-new-fire-chief/)

## Rescue Mission: need up 70 percent
The Lexington Rescue Mission says it is serving about 70 percent more people than a year ago — over 400 meals a day, up from roughly 250 — and has already spent its food budget for the year. It has set a goal of 6,500 meals this month. Its community Thanksgiving dinner is Wednesday, Nov. 25, from 3 to 6 p.m. [LEX 18](https://www.lex18.com/news/covering-kentucky/lexington-rescue-mission-surge-thanksgiving-meal-challenge-nov-19-2026)

## A new plan for snow days
After two winters of complaints, the city has laid out a revised snow plan: more drivers, lower-ranked residential streets combined into one tier, the National Weather Service as the main forecast source, and a "Plan B" with Fayette County Public Schools to move bus stops onto cleared roads. Officials say not every street can be treated at once, and they'll review the plan after the first snowfall. [WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/19/lexington-outlines-new-winter-storm-plan-after-two-years-criticism/)
