## Series history
Kentucky leads 33-25; tonight is meeting No. 59. Kentucky won the last one, 72-60 at Rupp on Dec. 13, 2025. The last game in Indiana was Dec. 10, 2011 — Christian Watford's buzzer-beating 3 for a 73-72 Indiana win over No. 1 Kentucky. The teams played 10 times at the old RCA Dome between 1987 and 2005; the new deal continues in Lexington in 2027 and Bloomington in 2028. ## Learn one thing: free-throw rate
![Indiana ranks 26th nationally at getting to the free-throw line; Kentucky ranks 339th](img/2026-11-20-ft-rate.svg)

Free-throw rate is free-throw attempts divided by field-goal attempts — how often a team turns a possession into trips to the line. It matters because free throws are the most efficient shot in basketball (about 70% go in, with the clock stopped), and because every attempt usually means a foul on the other team. Indiana lives there: about 31 attempts a game, 26th in the country. Kentucky gets there less often than almost anyone — 339th.

**Try it tonight:** keep a rough tally of team fouls each half. Once a team reaches its 7th foul of a half, the other side shoots one-and-one; at 10, two shots every time. If Kentucky's big men pick up two fouls early, watch how Pope adjusts — and whether Indiana keeps driving at them.

## Why it matters
Kentucky's identity under Pope has been shooting and spacing; Indiana's is size, rebounding and fouls drawn. Whichever team gets the game played its way — threes and clean defense, or second chances and free throws — probably wins in a cavernous building where shooting backgrounds are unfamiliar to both.
