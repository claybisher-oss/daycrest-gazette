## Officer-involved shooting on Ashmont Way
Lexington police officers responding Friday evening to a reported armed robbery in the 4800 block of Ashmont Way were involved in a shooting. Police said no responding officers were injured; they have not released information about the person involved. Lexington Police asked Kentucky State Police to investigate, as is standard practice, and the Fayette County coroner was called to the scene. Police asked people to avoid the area while the scene is active. Anyone with information can call Lexington Police at (859) 258-3600 or Bluegrass Crime Stoppers at (859) 253-2020. [WKYT, Nov. 20](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/20/lexington-officer-involved-on-duty-shooting/)

## This weekend around the Bluegrass
- **Kentucky Castle Holiday Market** (Versailles) — 20-plus vendors, treats and a hot-chocolate bar; Saturday and Sunday, 10 a.m.–6 p.m. Tickets required: $22 adults, $10 ages 3–11.
- **"White Christmas"** — Lexington Theatre Company at the Lexington Opera House: Saturday 1 and 7:30 p.m., Sunday 1 and 6:30 p.m.
- **First Winter Art Market at Josephine Sculpture Park** (3355 Lawrenceburg Road, Frankfort) — free; about 20 artisans, live music, s'mores, sculpture trails and a wood-carving demonstration. Starts Saturday at 1 p.m.
[WKYT Weekend Roundup, Nov. 20](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/20/weekend-roundup-kentucky-castle-holiday-market-white-christmas-winter-art-market/)

## A trillion-dollar holiday
The Kentucky Retail Federation is urging shoppers to buy local this season. Nationally, the National Retail Federation forecasts November–December sales of about $1.013 trillion — up 4.2% from 2025 and the first time over $1 trillion — while its survey expects about 182.7 million people to shop over Thanksgiving weekend, slightly fewer than last year's 186.9 million. [WKYT, Nov. 20](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/20/kentucky-retail-federation-encourages-shoppers-support-local-businesses-this-holiday-season/)
