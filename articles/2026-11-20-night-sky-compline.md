## Sky tonight
The Moon is a waxing gibbous, 79% lit and ten days into its cycle, riding in Aries. It sits in the southeast in early evening and stays up most of the night. Thin clouds come and go, so look for it between breaks. It reaches full — November's "Beaver Moon" — on Tuesday, Nov. 24, at 2:53 p.m. Eastern, so Monday and Tuesday nights will both look nearly full. ## To close the day
*Psalm 134* — a short night psalm for the servants of the Lord who stand in his house by night: lift up your hands and bless the Lord, and may the Lord who made heaven and earth bless you from Zion.

A collect for the night (Book of Common Prayer, Compline, public domain): *Be present, O merciful God, and protect us through the hours of this night, so that we who are wearied by the changes and chances of this life may rest in your eternal changelessness; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.*
