## The argument
Sacha Mugisha starts from a real hunger: many young men online are drawn to "vitalism," the Nietzsche-flavored ideal popularized by the pseudonymous book *Bronze Age Mindset*, which pictures manhood as an archaic warrior's pursuit of power. Mugisha grants that the craving for vitality is real — but says it's being sought in the wrong age. He traces Christian hopes for a "new Pentecost" from Pope John XXIII's call for renewal through the Catholic charismatic movement that followed, including Fr. George Montague's 1974 account of students who, after meeting the Spirit, took up humble household service. With Kierkegaard and Raniero Cantalamessa, he argues that life in the Spirit comes by mortification patterned on Christ, who laid down his life so others could live. The answer to a lifeless culture, he concludes, is the Church Age — people surrendered to the Holy Spirit — not a myth of domination. [Mere Orthodoxy, Nov. 20](https://mereorthodoxy.com/church-age-mindset/)
