## The Gobble Grease Toss moves
Lexington's free holiday cooking-oil recycling event, now in its 17th year, has a new home: the city's Electronics Recycling Center at 1306 Versailles Road. It runs Monday, Nov. 30, through Saturday, Dec. 5 — 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday; noon to 4 p.m. on Wednesday; and 8 a.m. to noon on Saturday. It's for Fayette County households, not businesses. Bring cooled oil in a hard-sided container with a tight lid and stay in your car; staff unload it. Motor oil, food scraps and bagged oil aren't accepted. The oil becomes biofuel through a partnership of UK's Center for Applied Energy Research, Kelley Green Biofuel and the city. Questions: LexCall 311. [WKYT, Nov. 20](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/20/lexingtons-gobble-grease-toss-moves-new-location-17th-year/)

## A coat to keep the cold away
Believing in Forever Inc., a Lexington nonprofit, is running its 13th annual youth coat drive. The goal is more than 2,000 new coats for children from newborns through high school, handed out through family resource centers across Central and Eastern Kentucky. Coats can be dropped off or shipped to First Baptist Church of Bracktown. Founder Devine Carama plans a 100-mile "Walk for Warmth" from Salyersville to Lexington next week, hoping to bring in 1,000 more coats before Black Friday. [WKYT, Nov. 20](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/20/believing-forever-inc-continues-annual-coat-drive/)

## Why it matters
Turkey-fryer oil poured down a drain clogs pipes and sewers; a free drop-off turns it into fuel instead. And new coats, given through school family resource centers, reach kids quietly and where they are.
