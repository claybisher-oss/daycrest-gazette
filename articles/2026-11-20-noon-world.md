## The summit
British Prime Minister Andy Burnham met European Council President António Costa and Commission President Ursula von der Leyen in Brussels today — only the second EU–U.K. summit since Britain left the bloc. The package on the table includes a youth-mobility program letting up to 100,000 young adults live in the other's territory for up to three years, a link-up of emissions-trading systems, and fewer checks on food and farm goods crossing the border. Burnham says it could add about half a percent to Britain's economy. An EU official said the deal still depends on settling university tuition fees within six months, and Burnham called rejoining the EU far from imminent. ## Why it matters
Ten years after the Brexit vote, the practical costs — paperwork at the border, fewer chances for young people to work abroad — are what both sides are trying to fix, piece by piece, without reopening the bigger question.
