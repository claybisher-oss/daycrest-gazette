## How to know it
A large, broad-winged hawk with a pale chest and a streaky "belly band" across the middle. Adults show a rusty-red tail when they bank in the sun; young birds have brown, banded tails. Most often seen perched still on a light pole, fence post or bare tree beside a field, watching for mice and voles.

## How to hear it
A hoarse, falling scream — *kee-eeee-arr*. It's the sound film-makers dub in for eagles.

## Where to find it locally
Watch the poles along US-68 and US-27 between Wilmore, Nicholasville and Lexington, the open fields around Shaker Village, and the farmland edges near Raven Run. Winter is easiest: leaves are down and northern birds join our year-round residents.

## A fun fact
Red-tails often mate for life and reuse the same big stick nest year after year, adding to it each spring.
