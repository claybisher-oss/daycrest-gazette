## Moon and sky
Tonight's moon looks like a lopsided full moon, high in the southeast after supper. Its glare will wash out faint stars all week, but Orion is clearing the eastern horizon by about 9 p.m. — look for the three belt stars in a short, slanted line.

## Weather lore
"A ring around the moon means rain soon." There's real science in it: the halo is moonlight bending through ice crystals in thin, high cirrus clouds, and those clouds often run ahead of an approaching front by a day or so.

## Today in Christian history
On Nov. 21, 1638, a General Assembly of the Church of Scotland meeting in Glasgow abolished government by bishops and put in place Presbyterian government — local elders, presbyteries and assemblies. It opened the Covenanter era and shaped churches far beyond Scotland. Also on this date, in 1866, the South African minister Tiyo Soga finished his translation of *The Pilgrim's Progress* into Xhosa. ([Christian History Institute](https://christianhistoryinstitute.org/today/11/21))

## Why it matters
How a church is governed shapes how it disciples people and how it handles disagreement — a live question every generation, including this one.
