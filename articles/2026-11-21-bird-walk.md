## Where
Any edge where a field meets woods works. One good Central Kentucky option: the Shawnee Run Trail to the Fulling Mill waterfall at Shaker Village of Pleasant Hill — a 1.4-mile loop with about 135 feet of climbing, past a creek and hedgerows (no dogs on this trail). [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/kentucky/shawnee-run-trail-to-fulling-mill-waterfall)

## Five birds to find
1. **Dark-eyed Junco** — slate gray with a white belly; flashes white tail feathers as it flies up (today's species).
2. **Carolina Wren** — a loud, rolling song far bigger than the bird.
3. **Tufted Titmouse** — small, gray, crested; a whistled *peter-peter-peter*.
4. **Eastern Bluebird** — soft blue backs on fence wires in open fields.
5. **Red-tailed Hawk** — perched on poles above pasture, rusty tail in the sun.

## How
Walk slowly, stop every hundred steps, and listen for a full minute before looking. Most winter birds are found by their call notes first.

## Why it matters
Paying attention is a skill; a bird walk is one of the gentlest ways to practice it.
