## Not a mirror image
In Romans 5:15–17 Paul sets Adam and Christ side by side — and then refuses to make them equals. In Greek the "trespass" (*paraptōma*) and the "free gift" (*charisma*) are two nouns with the same ending, so they sound alike. Paul's point is that they *aren't* alike: if one man's false step brought death to many, "how much more" (*pollō mallon*) does God's grace in one man, Jesus Christ, overflow to many. It's an argument from the lesser to the greater.
