## Council passes street-racing fines
The Urban County Council voted 12-1 for an ordinance that bans drag racing, speed contests, burnouts, donuts, drifting and wheelies on city streets. A first offense brings a $1,500 fine; a repeat offense brings $4,500 and a 60-day impoundment of the vehicle. The rules take effect July 1, follow a 2022 Louisville law, and are softer than the first draft. [WEKU](https://www.weku.org/lexington-richmond/2026-11-20/thinking-of-street-racing-in-lexington-starting-soon-offenders-will-face-heavy-fines)

## Fatal police shooting on Ashmont Way
Lexington police answering a reported robbery in progress in the 4800 block of Ashmont Way on Friday evening met a 38-year-old man armed with a knife, and an officer fired, according to Chief Lawrence Weathers. Officers gave aid, but the man died at the scene. No officers were hurt; Kentucky State Police are investigating, and neither the officer nor the man has been named. [WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/21/law-enforcement-reveals-more-details-about-officer-involved-shooting-lexington/)

## City government center now over $97 million
Renovating the former Truist Bank building on West Vine Street into a new city government center is now expected to cost more than $97 million — at least $11 million above June's estimate — with the yearly lease payment up to about $4.1 million. The council, which approved the project 8-7 last December, could hear new lease terms as soon as Dec. 1. [WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/20/cost-lexingtons-new-city-government-center-rises-by-more-than-11-million/)

Also: Mayor Linda Gorton named Assistant Chief Joey Harris, a 25-year veteran, as Lexington's next fire chief when Jason Wells retires in January. [WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/19/lexington-mayor-announces-new-fire-chief/)
