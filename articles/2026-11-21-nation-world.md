## The Asia trip
At a White House briefing, President Trump called his Asia trip a success, pointing to "joint guardrails" on artificial intelligence agreed with China's Xi Jinping and a trade document signed with Thailand. NPR reports that the first three-way meeting with Xi and Russia's Vladimir Putin produced only an agreement to keep talking about reducing nuclear risk, and details of the AI arrangement haven't been released. Inside the administration, officials disagree over chip sales to China. [NPR via WEKU](https://www.weku.org/npr-news/2026-11-20/trump-touts-meeting-with-xi-and-putin-but-offers-little-results)

## Britain and the EU draw closer
Prime Minister Andy Burnham met EU leaders in Brussels on a package that includes a youth mobility plan for up to 100,000 young adults, linked emissions trading, and fewer checks on food and farm goods. Supporters say it could add 0.4% to Britain's economy; Brexit backers object that Britain would follow rules it doesn't help write. [NPR/AP via WEKU](https://www.weku.org/npr-news/2026-11-20/the-u-k-and-eu-hold-a-rare-summit-to-strengthen-ties)

## A village moves uphill
The Quinault Indian Nation is moving its coastal village of Taholah, Washington, inland as higher tides top its seawall. The first four families move in January, with 32 more homes planned next year; the Nation has spent more than $40 million and needs far more. [NPR via WEKU](https://www.weku.org/npr-news/2026-11-20/rising-seas-have-forced-this-quinault-village-to-relocate)
