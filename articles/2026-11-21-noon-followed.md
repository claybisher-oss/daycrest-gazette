## Front Porch Republic — "Chastity, Immortality, and Video Games"
Jeffrey Bilbro's weekend reading list opens with Nic Rowan's essay on writers who take a "vow" never to use AI — a stand Bilbro admires and calls beautifully doomed. The rest of the list ranges over AI and intellectual life, the tech world's pursuit of immortality, video-game escapism, parents tracking their kids, and Wendell Berry on what machines do to work. A good map of the week's arguments about technology and limits, with links to each piece. [Read it (~5 min)](https://www.frontporchrepublic.com/2026/11/chastity-immortality-and-video-games/)

## Seedbed — "Ask Me about My Band" (Romans 15:14–22)
J.D. Walt describes his weekly Discipleship Band — a few friends who meet to pray and confess together — and argues that this kind of committed friendship changes people more than more knowledge does. He ties it to Paul's picture of believers being made into an offering acceptable to God. [Read it (~4 min)](https://seedbed.com/rom-ask-me-about-my-band/)
