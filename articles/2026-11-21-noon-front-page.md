## South Limestone closes overnight
South Limestone between Cooper Drive and Virginia Avenue closes from **7 PM tonight to 6 AM Sunday** while crews install a pedestrian bridge for UK's hospital. Sunday night is held in reserve if the work runs long. The west sidewalk is closed, and a temporary crosswalk is in place. Plan around it if you're headed to campus or downtown tonight. ## Woodford County field-trip bus crashes in Lexington
A Woodford County school bus carrying students on a field trip crashed in Lexington Friday. Crews checked every student and staff member; the only reported injury was the driver, who was taken to a hospital by ambulance. The district sent another bus to finish the trip. [WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/21/1-sent-hospital-after-crash-involving-woodford-county-school-bus-lexington/)

## Coroner identifies man killed in police shooting
The Fayette County coroner identified the man killed by a Lexington police officer Friday afternoon in the 4800 block of Ashmont Way as Justin Burrows, 38. Officers were sent about 4:30 PM to a reported robbery in progress; police say the first officer to arrive met a man armed with knives. That officer, the only one who fired, was wearing a recording body camera and is on administrative assignment. Kentucky State Police are investigating. [LEX 18](https://www.lex18.com/news/covering-kentucky/justin-burrows-man-killed-ashmont-way-shooting-lexington-police-officer-nov-21-2026) · [WKYT](https://www.wkyt.com/2026/11/21/law-enforcement-reveals-more-details-about-officer-involved-shooting-lexington/)
