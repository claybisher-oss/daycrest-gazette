## Learn one thing: how an NFL drive works
![Diagram of an NFL drive: each new set of downs marked along the field, ending in the end zone](img/2026-11-21-nfl-drive.svg)
*Diagram: Daycrest Gazette*

A drive is one team's string of plays from the moment it gets the ball until it scores, punts or turns it over. Every first down buys four more tries to gain 10 yards; on fourth down the coach chooses between punting, kicking a field goal or going for it — and where the ball sits on the field drives that choice. Near midfield, a punt usually wins; inside the opponent's 35, a field goal is in play. Late in each half, the two-minute warning stops the clock and resets the game into hurry-up mode. The Bengals have scored about 31 points a game this season (278 in nine games, by our count from NFL.com scores), and Burrow is completing 74% of his passes with a 114.8 rating — so their drives tend to end in points. [CBS Sports stats](https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/teams/cin/cincinnati-bengals/stats/)

**Try it Monday:** for each Bengals drive, write where it started and how it ended. Count how many drives starting past their own 35 end in points.

## Why it matters
Monday night is the cleanest look yet at the Bengals' offense against a team with a losing record — a good game for learning to read drives.
