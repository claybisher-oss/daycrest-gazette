## Football: tonight in Columbia
Kentucky (7-3, 5-3 SEC; No. 16 in the CFP rankings, No. 17 AP) plays Missouri (6-4, 3-4) at **7:30 PM ET on SEC Network** at Faurot Field. Both teams have lost two straight; Missouri is about a 5-point favorite. Today's separate **Game Day** post has the coaches, injuries, players to know, a matchup card, a 3-minute audio preview and a Learn One Thing on the line of scrimmage. ## Men's basketball: the morning after Indiana
The Herald-Leader's Noah Fleischman looked at why Kentucky keeps failing to finish games after Friday's 90-76 loss — the Cats led at halftime but lost the second half 45-29 and attempted just 7 free throws to Indiana's 31. Mark Pope's postgame line: "We're in the process right now." National outlets are already writing about pressure on Pope in his second season. Next: Wichita State at Rupp, Friday, 9 PM, ESPN2. [Herald-Leader via Yahoo](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/uk-basketball-isn-t-finishing-053107756.html) · [Saturday Down South](https://www.saturdaydownsouth.com/news/college-basketball/mark-popes-seat-reaches-boiling-point-as-kentucky-falls-to-indiana/)
