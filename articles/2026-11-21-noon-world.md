## Kryvyi Rih
A Russian ballistic-missile attack on Kryvyi Rih — President Volodymyr Zelenskyy's hometown in central Ukraine — killed a 7-year-old boy and a 20-year-old woman Friday night and wounded about 60 people, six of them children. About 25 apartment buildings, a hospital and schools were damaged. Hours earlier, two Russian missiles and a suspected drone came down in Poland, a NATO member. Zelenskyy sharply criticized Vladimir Putin after President Trump, at a summit in China, called the Russian leader a friend. ## A burning ship in the Gulf of Finland
The Sparta II, a cargo ship the U.S. has sanctioned for ties to Russia's military logistics, caught fire after a suspected drone strike near Gogland Island and drifted into Finnish waters. All 16 crew stayed aboard, and no pollution had been found. Finland reported a suspected Russian helicopter entering its airspace near Loviisa and restored restrictions in the eastern gulf. Ukraine has not claimed the attack.
