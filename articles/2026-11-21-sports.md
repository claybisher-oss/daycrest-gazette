## Bengals
Cincinnati (7-2) sits alone atop the AFC North after a 38-18 win over Pittsburgh last Sunday, when Joe Burrow threw for 409 yards and 4 touchdowns and Ja'Marr Chase caught passes for 231 yards — a franchise single-game record. Next: **at Washington (3-5-1), Monday, Nov. 23, 8:15 p.m., ESPN.** A win would give Zac Taylor 64 career wins, tying Sam Wyche for second in team history. ## Sports 101 (NFL): how a drive works
An offense gets four downs to gain 10 yards; make it and the "chains" reset to a new first down. A drive is every play from when a team gets the ball until it scores, punts or turns it over. The Bengals lead the league in passing but rank 30th in rushing — so watch Monday for how often they gain the 10 yards in two big chunks instead of four small ones. **Try it Monday:** on the Bengals' first drive, count the plays and the first downs.
