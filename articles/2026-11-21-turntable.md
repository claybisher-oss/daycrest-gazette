## Recommendation of the day
**Josh Garrels — *Love & War & The Sea In Between* (2011).** A long, generous record that moves between folk, soul and hip-hop rhythms, all of it about longing, grace and the troubled water between them. Garrels gave it away free when it came out. Best heard start to finish on a drive. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/6JyBFqPzadKzBwCY7A1PUo)

## New to You
**"White Owl" — Josh Garrels** (about 6 minutes). If you've been playing hushed indie folk with a spiritual undertow — Isakov's quiet guitar, Jon Guerra's faith-shaped writing — this sits right between them, with a patient build that rewards headphones. [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/1bqnR4LChgaQ2H4U0yipaS)

## Why it matters
Some records are best as a whole; a Saturday drive is the right length for one.
