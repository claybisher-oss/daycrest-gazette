## Indiana 90, Kentucky 76
Kentucky led 47-45 at halftime after a 12-0 run, but Indiana won the second half 45-29 before 23,078 at Lucas Oil Stadium. The swing came early after the break: with Milan Momcilovic on the bench, Indiana ran off 8 straight points in under a minute. Momcilovic led all scorers with 28 (5-of-10 from three); Zoom Diallo added 15, Alex Wilkins 13 and Malachi Moreno 12 with 7 rebounds. Bryce Lindsey had 20 for Indiana. The gap that decided it: Indiana made 28 of 31 free throws to Kentucky's 3 of 7, outscored UK's bench 33-4 and outrebounded the Cats 35-27. Kentucky is 4-2. ## Learn one thing: free-throw rate
When one team shoots 31 free throws and the other shoots 7, the game is often decided before the shot chart. Free-throw rate (free-throw attempts ÷ field-goal attempts) measures how often a team gets to the line — a sign of attacking the rim and of the other team's fouling. **Try it Friday:** count Kentucky's trips to the line in the first 10 minutes against Wichita State.
